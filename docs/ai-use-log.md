**Policy notes we are following:**

- Never invent data, findings, citations, units, or metadata. Anything the SI tool isn't 100% sure of goes into the Unknowns section of the relevant doc with a note of what to verify.
- No LLM-written text in the Decision Brief or UI; deterministic templates only, fed by JSON fields produced by tested Python.
- This log is updated the same day any SI tool is used; no retroactive reconstruction.
